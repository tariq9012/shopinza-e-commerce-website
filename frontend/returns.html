Returns & Refunds

Last updated September 2026

30-Day Return Window

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as the item is unused and in its original packaging.

How to Start a Return

  • Go to My Orders and find the delivered order.
  • Contact us with your Order ID and the reason for the return.
  • We'll confirm pickup or drop-off details within 1 business day.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund is processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method. Cash on Delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer or JazzCash/Easypaisa.

Order Cancellation (before delivery)

Changed your mind before the order even ships? You don't need to wait for a return - just cancel it directly from My Orders while it's still Pending or Paid, and it's instant.

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene reasons, opened skincare/beauty products cannot be returned unless defective.
