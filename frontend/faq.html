Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us and we'll help you out.

How long does delivery take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (available at checkout for $25) arrives in 1-2 business days. You'll receive an email as soon as your order ships, and you can always check the live status from My Orders.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Credit/Debit cards (via Stripe), JazzCash, Easypaisa, and Cash on Delivery. All online payments are processed securely - we never store your card details on our servers.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Yes - as long as your order hasn't shipped yet. Go to My Orders and click "Cancel Order" on any order that's still Pending or Paid. Once an order ships, it can no longer be self-cancelled; please contact us instead.

How do I return an item?

See our full Returns & Refunds page for the step-by-step process and eligibility window.

Do you ship internationally?

Right now we only ship within Pakistan. We're working on expanding - check back soon!

I have a coupon code - how do I use it?

Add your items to the cart, then on the Checkout page enter your code in the "Coupon code" box and click Apply. The discount will be reflected in your order total immediately.

How do I track my order?

Log in and visit My Orders to see the live status of every order you've placed - it updates automatically the moment we ship it, no need to refresh.
