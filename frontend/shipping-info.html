Shipping Info

Last updated September 2026

Shipping Methods & Timelines

  • Standard Shipping - Free, arrives in 3-5 business days.
  • Express Shipping - $25, arrives in 1-2 business days.

Orders placed before 2 PM are usually processed the same day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll get an email confirmation. You can also check the live status any time from My Orders - it updates automatically the moment we mark it as shipped or delivered, no need to refresh the page.

Shipping Coverage

We currently ship to all major cities and towns across Pakistan. Delivery times may be slightly longer for remote areas.

Delayed or Missing Orders

If your order hasn't arrived within the expected window, please contact us with your Order ID and we'll look into it right away.
