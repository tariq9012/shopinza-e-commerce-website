Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Shopinza collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account info: name, email address, and (if you sign up with Google/Facebook) your public profile name and email from that provider.
  • Order info: shipping address, phone number, and order history.
  • Payment info: we never store your full card number - payments are processed directly by Stripe, JazzCash, or Easypaisa.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To respond to support requests
  • To send newsletter emails, only if you've subscribed

Data Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text - they're hashed. Access tokens expire automatically, and sensitive actions (checkout, account changes) always require you to be signed in.

Your Choices

You can update your account details any time, unsubscribe from the newsletter with one click, and request account deletion by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.
