Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take? expand_more

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping arrives in 1-2 business days for an additional fee.

What is your return policy? expand_more

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, as long as items are unused and in original packaging.

Do you ship internationally? expand_more

Yes, we currently ship to over 30 countries. Shipping costs and delivery times are calculated at checkout.

How can I track my order? expand_more

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email. You can also check order status from your account page.
